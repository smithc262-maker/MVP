Welcome to my website! Here you can find answers to common questions about our campus.

Places You Will Need to Know

  • The mail room is located on the second floor of the Drysdale Student Center.
  • Counselers usually have their offices in either Hundley Hall or Carnegie Hall.
  • The library has study rooms on the first floor and a computer lab that can be accessed even when the library is closed.
  • The fitness room is on the bottom floor of Turner Gymnasium, the most direct way to it is the stairs on the side of the building.
  • The Domino's Pizza on Campus is located on the bottom floor of Rainsford Hall.
